Our Daily Obsession

We believe a great loaf of bread requires only three ingredients: flour, water, and sea salt.

Every loaf at Hearth & Rise Sourdough starts with organic flour grown by family farmers in the Pacific Northwest. We nurture our signature wild yeast starter daily, allowing it to ferment naturally for 36 hours. The result is a deeply complex flavor, an open airy crumb, and a crisp, dark crust that sings when sliced.
