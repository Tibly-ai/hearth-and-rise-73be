Our Heritage & Mission

How we brought honest bread back to our neighborhood.

We founded Hearth & Rise Sourdough with a simple commitment to slow down, return to clean culinary roots, and feed our local community bread that is nutritious, deeply flavorful, and naturally preserved.

Every morning, we roll up our sleeves at 3:00 AM while the streets are still dark. Using only heritage flours sourced directly from independent farmers, filtered water, hand-harvested sea salt, and our active wild starter, we work with nature to produce loaves that stay fresh and taste of the earth.

Natural wild fermentation process

Our 72-Hour Slow Fermentation Method

We do not rush our sourdough. True depth of flavor and easy digestion can only be achieved by allowing natural wild yeast to develop at its own pace over three full days.

1

Sourcing Local Heritage Grain

We collaborate directly with independent Oregon growers. Our flour is stone ground in small batches, retaining the wheat berry germ and bran where all the nutritional benefits and complex soil flavors live.

2

The 72-Hour Rise

Our wild starter, born right here in the bakery, is nurtured with gentle feedings. The dough undergoes long cold autolyse and bulk fermentation, unlocking nutrients and breaking down gluten for a gut friendly digestion.

3

Baked on Fire & Stone

Loaves are baked at high heat directly on the stone hearth of our deck ovens. Steam creates the classic open structure, deep caramelized blistered crust, and perfect soft chew you look for in authentic bread.
