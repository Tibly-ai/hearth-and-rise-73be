The Sourdough Journey

Crafting great bread cannot be rushed. Our process is a commitment to old-world techniques, clean grains, and patience.

1

Wild Culture

We feed our sourdough starter with raw flour and pure spring water twice a day to build deep enzymatic structure.

2

Long Autolyse

Flour and water rest together for a full hour before adding salt, letting the flour absorb moisture naturally.

3

Cold Rise

Each hand-shaped loaf rests in willow proofing baskets in our cold room for twenty-four hours to develop flavor.

4

Deck Baking

The intense heat from our heavy slate-deck oven instantly caramelizes the wild crust to an amber finish.
